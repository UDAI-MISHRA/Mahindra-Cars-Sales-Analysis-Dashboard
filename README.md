# 🚙🚘 Mahindra cars Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Mahindra cars Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project designed to analyze and visualize the sales performance of Mahindra Thar vehicles.

This dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales, revenue, profitability, customer ratings, vehicle models, sales channels, and regional performance through visually appealing charts and KPI cards.

The dashboard features a professional automotive-inspired black and red theme, making business insights easy to understand.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Track total profit and discount amounts.
- Compare sales across different Thar models.
- Identify top-performing regions.
- Analyze customer ratings and satisfaction.
- Understand sales distribution by variant and fuel type.
- Monitor monthly sales trends.
- Compare performance across sales channels.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following important KPIs:

| Total Sales | 9.77bn |

| Average Delivery Days | 17.59 |

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes:

1. **Total Sales by Month** – Monthly sales performance and trends.
2. **Total Sales by Model** – Comparison of sales across different Thar models.
3. **India Sales Map** – Geographical distribution of sales across India.
4. **Sales Channel Analysis** – Sales contribution from different channels.
5. **Units Sold by Model** – Comparison of units sold for each vehicle.
6. **Sales by Region** – Regional sales performance.
7. **Average Customer Rating** – Customer rating comparison by model.
8. **Sales by Variant** – Analysis of Base, Mid, and Top variants.
9. **Sales by Fuel Type** – Comparison of Diesel, Petrol, and Electric categories.
